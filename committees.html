Committees

HandsOn San Diego is dedicated to volunteer action—so much so that we are a volunteer-powered organization. As such, we are always looking for additional committee members. If you are interested becoming a committee member, submit an interest form today!

Each committee meets at least once a month virtually and has representatives from our staff and advisory board present.

Our committees include Governance, Volunteer Engagement, Marketing, and Development—explore each committee below.

Governance Committee

If you have an interest in working directly with the Advisory Board of a local nonprofit, we encourage you to check out the Governance Committee!

In this role, volunteers will be working together to uphold and strengthen the governance structure of HandsOn San Diego. To ensure that the board of directors is in alignment with our mission, values, and ethical standards, committee members will be monitoring the effectiveness of the board, developing relevant policies, ensuring regulatory compliance, supporting leadership planning, and more! 

For full role details, check out our Governance Committee Member Description.

Volunteer Engagement Committee

Are you passionate about event planning, community building, or volunteer engagement? HandsOn San Diego is inviting dynamic and motivated individuals to join our Volunteer Engagement Committee!

This group plays a key role in creating and coordinating our signature events—VolunBEERS and VolunCHEERS—which strengthen community connections and celebrate volunteerism across San Diego. As a committee member, you'll gain hands-on experience in event planning, fundraising, volunteer engagement, and community partnership development.

For full role details, check out our Volunteer Engagement Committee Member Description.

Marketing Committee

Calling all creatives! Whether you have an eye for design, a talent for weaving stories, creating marketing and outreach strategies, or documenting stories and footage at projects – there’s a place for you on our Marketing Committee!

As a committee member, you’ll play a key role by being the voice of our organization through marketing and outreach strategies. Through collaboration efforts across the board, members work to create social media campaigns, identify gaps in our marketing and outreach efforts, and utilize their skills to positively impact our community.

If you’re excited to make your mark in creative ways, we encourage you to apply! For full role details, check out our Marketing Committee Member Description.

Development Committee

Are you passionate about fundraising, grant writing, or building strategic partnerships? HandsOn San Diego is looking for driven and community-minded individuals to join our Development Committee—a group focused on strengthening the foundation of our mission through creative and sustainable resource development.

As a committee member, you’ll play a key role in identifying funding opportunities, cultivating relationships with businesses and government agencies, supporting individual giving campaigns, and contributing to the planning of annual events like San Diego Gives and Global Volunteer Month. You’ll also help refine our case for support and build momentum for ongoing and future fundraising efforts.

For full role details, check out our Development Committee Member Description.
